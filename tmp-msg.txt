perf: the registry no longer re-enters its own permission check

Reading a registry key asked the permission check whether the calling app
may touch it, and the permission grants now live in the registry - so
reading the grants asked the registry, which asked for the grants, and the
two asked each other until the stack gave out. Every read cost about
300ms, which is why the Registry Editor froze for a second on every click.

- a finished migration is marked BEFORE it runs, and the marker is written
  without the permission check, so a migration can never re-enter itself
- only an app that is actually on the stack counts as the caller: a call
  from the shell's own modules is the shell's business, whatever app
  happens to be focused (otherwise a focused app could block the OS's own
  registry writes)
- the registry reads its own bookkeeping (the app registrations) and the
  grants through unchecked readers

Measured in the browser: a click in the Registry Editor went from 3190ms
to 4.4ms, and 50 registry reads from 5.9s to 0.6ms.

Also: the build scripts now accept either line endings, since git may check
sw.js out with CRLF.
