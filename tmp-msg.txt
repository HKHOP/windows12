feat: the registry becomes the OS's settings store

The registry was the install index and an app sandbox; now the OS keeps
its own state in it too, Windows-style, instead of a handful of separate
JSON files:

- HKCU\Software\Windows 12\Shell      the current profile's settings
- HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\Video  the device's display settings
- HKCU\Software\Windows 12\Permissions\<uuid>  the per-app permission grants
- HKCR\<ext>                          the default app for a file type

HKCU is really per profile now: two people signed in at the same time have
their own hives, their own settings and their own grants, and the Registry
Editor shows exactly that.

The old files are migrated into the registry once (the markers live in the
registry itself) and are not written any more: the per-user config.json,
the device's display/settings.json, the permissions/grants.json and the
fileAssociations/defaults.json.
